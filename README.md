## About

I'm a **Mobile & Full-Stack Developer** from Indonesia and the founder of **[Buketmu](https://buketmu.com)**, a website builder that 120+ florists now use to sell online. By day I build the Android TV in-room entertainment system at **LifeMedia**, which runs in the rooms of 10+ hotel clients. Before that I shipped a Flutter app for the Bontang city government and rebuilt the Narajiwa Android app.

Most of my work is **Kotlin and Flutter** on the client, with **Laravel**, React, and Docker on the server when a product needs its own backend. I also teach: I was a teaching assistant for Mobile App Development at Universitas Sebelas Maret, where I study Informatics.
